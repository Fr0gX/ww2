# Component Batch 01 — Final Prompt Set

All assets use the built-in image generation mode. The V6 visual master is a style reference only.

## Plain base

One single reusable flat-top hexagonal plain-grass ground tile matching V6's simplified shape language. Square canvas, transparent outside the exact hex silhouette. Orthographic high three-quarter projection consistent with a 55-degree map camera. Centered with transparent padding. Simple opaque hand-painted game art, flat fresh grass green, at most three tiny darker grass brush marks. All six edges perfectly straight and clean; uniform edge color; no rim, bevel, outline, border, shadow, grid, scenery, text, or watermark.

## Forest overlay

One compact forest overlay for the center of a flat-top hex cell, viewed with the same high three-quarter camera. Square canvas with genuinely transparent background; no tile or ground. A sparse cluster of simplified evergreen masses, each built from two or three broad dark-green shapes and one minimal trunk hint. One base color, one shadow plane, and one highlight plane. Wide transparent safety margin; no needles, branches, bark, texture, outlines, gradients, individual leaves, text, or watermark.

## Hill overlay

One low rounded hill overlay centered in a flat-top hex cell. Square canvas with genuinely transparent background; no tile or ground. One broad low hill made from exactly three shapes: warm olive base, darker shadow plane, and small lighter crest plane. Wide transparent margin; no outline, gradient, grass blades, rocks, soil texture, erosion lines, noise, scenery, text, or watermark.

## Mountain overlay

One simplified mountain overlay centered in a flat-top hex cell. Square canvas with genuinely transparent background; no tile, ground, sky, or scenery. One compact triangular mountain made from three large painted planes: cool gray base, deep blue-gray shadow, and a small pale summit plane. Wide transparent margin; no outline, gradient, surface texture, cracks, rubble, tiny rocks, text, or watermark.

## Marsh overlay and extraction

Generate three broad muted blue-green shallow-water shapes and two tiny three-stroke reed clusters, centered with a wide transparent margin. Use only water, shadow-accent, and reed colors. No tile, ground, outline, gradient, reflections, ripples, mud, scenery, text, or watermark.

The generated checker background was removed with a background-extraction edit: preserve the marsh shapes and colors exactly; remove every background pixel; output genuine RGBA alpha transparency with no checker pattern, white matte, halo, shadow, text, or watermark.
