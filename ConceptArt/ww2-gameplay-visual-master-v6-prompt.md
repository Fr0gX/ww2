# WW2 Gameplay Visual Master V6 — Generation Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: brand-new 16:9 gameplay screenshot and style master for a modular hex-grid World War II PC strategy game

Primary request: create a genuinely simple, clean, stylized hand-painted game screen from scratch. Simplification must apply to every object, not only the UI. The map should read instantly from large shapes and color, with very little descriptive detail.

Visual language: opaque digital paint with flat, confident shapes and slightly imperfect hand-painted edges. No outlines. Each object uses at most three value steps: one base color, one shadow shape, one small highlight. No gradients, realistic material rendering, texture noise, or surface detail. The result is graphic and iconic, but still warm and handmade rather than vector-perfect.

Strict detail budget:

- Grass hex: one flat green field plus at most three short brush marks.
- Farmland hex: three to five broad parallel crop bands, no individual plants.
- Forest overlay: three to five simple overlapping tree masses, no branches or needles.
- Rock overlay: two or three faceted stone shapes, no cracks or granular texture.
- Mountain: one bold triangular mass with two value planes.
- River: one clean blue ribbon with one highlight shape, no ripples.
- Road: one simple warm path with no gravel texture.
- City: four to six block-like building masses and one unmistakable center landmark; no individual windows, roof tiles, bricks, chimneys, street clutter, or dense rows of houses.
- City wall: plain repeatable wall bars and gate shapes; no masonry detail.
- Soldier: four to six major silhouette shapes; no facial features, clothing folds, buttons, or weapon detail beyond the recognizable silhouette.
- Tank and artillery: iconic silhouettes with broad body, turret or barrel shapes; no rivets, individual track links, machinery, or weathering.

Modular construction: every base hex is an independent identical module. Ground paint stays within its cell. Roads and rivers connect only at exact edge midpoints. Forests, rocks, mountains, buildings, walls, bridges, and units are separate centered overlays. Cells meet seamlessly with no gaps, thickness, bevels, or rims. Show one very faint global shared-edge grid; never outline each hex separately.

Scene: a spacious temperate battlefield with plain grass as the dominant terrain, a few farmland cells, two small forest groups, two rock or mountain cells, one river and bridge, sparse roads, one friendly city, and one contested fortified city. Leave many cells visually quiet. Avoid filling the map.

Units: one blue infantry, one blue medic with a satchel silhouette, one blue light tank, and one blue field gun; a small opposing vermilion force near the enemy city. Each unit is a standalone high-three-quarter-view sprite with fixed scale and one compact contact shadow. No model bases and no realistic anatomy.

Gameplay state: select the blue infantry near the center. Show movement with clean translucent blue inner fills, supply with a thin green inner band, enemy control with a thin vermilion inner band, a simple center-to-center route, and one target marker at the city gate. Preserve the terrain colors beneath overlays.

Camera: high orthographic three-quarter view around 55 degrees, no horizon, no depth blur. Map fills at least 82% of the screen.

Clarity and color: crystal-clear air, neutral bright daylight, no fog or atmosphere. Use fresh separated colors: grass green, deep forest green, golden farmland, cool gray rock, clear blue water, steel-blue friendly units, vermilion enemies, amber selection. Maintain strong local contrast without becoming neon.

UI: extremely simple professional PC interface: slim navy top bar, small objective panel, narrow unit panel, tiny combat preview, compact square action buttons, and one end-turn button. Flat solid panels, one shadow, no borders within borders, no ornaments.

Text (verbatim): only “目标”, “补给”, “驻扎”, “占领城市”, “结束回合”. Clear compact Chinese sans-serif. Numerals and icons are allowed.

Constraints: continuous world assembled from a finite reusable hex kit; readable at normal gameplay zoom; no repeated linework; no national flags, real insignia, logos, swastikas, or watermark.

Avoid: realism, semi-realism, realistic architecture, realistic vegetation, detailed cityscape, detailed landscape, detailed soldiers, individual windows, bricks, roof tiles, foliage, rock texture, fabric texture, track links, watercolor, sketching, ink contours, fog, desaturation, dirt, old-map styling, tabletop board, miniatures, toy bases, glossy 3D, mobile-game card UI, fantasy, modern equipment.
